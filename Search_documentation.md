# Search documentation — ML experiment provenance systematic review

The systematic database search was closed on **16 February 2026**. The following search strings and record counts describe the **primary database search** and correspond to the four BibTeX export ZIP files in this repository.

## ACM Digital Library

Search strategy:

`(provenance OR "provenance tracking") AND ("machine learning" OR "deep learning" OR "data science") AND (workflow* OR pipeline* OR experiment* OR tracking OR system* OR script* OR analys*)`

Records exported (before deduplication): **226**.

## IEEE Xplore

Search strategy:

`(("provenance" OR "provenance tracking") AND ("machine learning") AND (workflow* OR tracking OR system*))`

Records exported (before deduplication): **234**. The original export is split across three BibTeX files in `ieee.zip`.

## ScienceDirect

Search strategy:

`("provenance" OR "lineage") AND ("machine learning" OR "deep learning") AND ("training" OR "hyperparameter" OR "evaluation" OR "preprocessing" OR "pipelines")`

Records exported (before deduplication): **148**. The original export is split across two BibTeX files in `sciencedirect.zip`.

## SpringerLink

Search strategy:

`("provenance" OR "provenance management") AND ("machine learning") AND (system* OR platform* OR software OR management)`

Records exported (before deduplication): **3**.

## Selection summary

- Primary database records: **611**.
- Duplicates removed: **4**.
- Unique records screened: **607**.
- Excluded at title/metadata stage: **567**.
- Reports assessed at full text: **40**.
- Full-text reports excluded: **3** (no eligible structured lineage: 1; non-primary vision/position: 2).
- Eligible primary-search reports: **37**.
- Additional eligible reports identified through publication-family tracing: **6**.
- **Final corpus: 43 primary reports describing 29 system families.**

## Supplementary coverage

Scopus was used separately as a supplementary check; no additional eligible primary study was reported from this check. No Scopus export is supplied, and Scopus records are not part of the 611-record primary search stream. Publication-family tracing identified reports associated with already eligible systems; its six additions are counted separately from the primary database records.
